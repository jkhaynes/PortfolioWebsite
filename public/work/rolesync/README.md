# RoleSync app screenshots

Captured in the RoleSync repo (`loot-membership-integration/artifacts/screenshots`, 2026-10-02) against the RoleSync development store and Discord server. Added to the portfolio on 2026-10-07, replacing the 2026-09-05 captures.

Each source capture is 1600 × 900. They were cropped to the app's content column (x 370 to 1230) with vertical bounds chosen so no card is cut off partway. No pixels were edited.

- `membership-tiers.png`: `02-membership-tiers.png`, 860 × 811. Silver, Gold and Bronze tiers with their Discord role, Shopify tag and priority.
- `dashboard.png`: `01-dashboard.png`, 860 × 865. Integration health, plan usage and customer counts. Counts describe the development store, not adoption or business impact.
- `staged-preview.png`: `05-staged-preview.png`, 860 × 811. Rename Shopify tag confirmation with affected customer counts.
- `sync-status.png`: `07-customers-attention.png`, 860 × 852. Customer sync status with one customer needing attention.
- `change-history.png`: `08-change-history.png`, 860 × 464. Change history list.

The settings and setup captures are not used because they show the development store's Shopify account ID in page URLs. No personal identifiers are visible in the images above; tier names, tags and the "RoleSync Dev" server name are development configuration.

Preserve these captures as app evidence. Do not relabel them as mockups. Use the shared media dialog to inspect the complete images without the homepage card's crop.
